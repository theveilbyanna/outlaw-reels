results, sales numbers, member counts, prices or features. Never show or describe Anna's face.
Everything is faceless: on-screen text carries the message, with the engine's music.

Rotation (one reel a day, set in `tools/reelq.py`): FrickFake → UAOM → The Veil → Outlaw Built Media → Veil Academy → Pocket Built.

## Caption rules (every reel)
Write `captions.json` with:
```json
{"facebook": "...", "tiktok": "...", "clapper": "...", "comment": "optional first comment",
 "link": "https://... (or the email for Outlaw Built Media)", "isAigc": false}
```
- Every caption has **SEO search keywords** (what people type into TikTok/Facebook search), **the link** written out, and **hashtags**.
- **facebook:** hook line, 2–5 short lines (emoji bullets are fine), the full https link (clickable on Facebook), a question that invites comments, 5–7 hashtags on the last line.
- **tiktok:** keyword-rich first line (TikTok search reads it), short body, "link in bio" plus the link written out, 6–10 hashtags on the last line.
- **clapper:** like TikTok but its own wording, 6–10 hashtags.
- **comment:** optional. A question or poll to pin.
- No hashtags in the middle of sentences. No fake urgency, no invented numbers.
